When I first found this company, I had reached the end of my rope and then some with trying to fix my dental health on my own. I have Sjogren’s syndrome which causes a very dry mouth among other things, so my oral health unfortunately took a dive the past several years despite meticulous oral hygiene. Tens of thousands of dollars of dental work later spent on fillings, root canals and crowns, I’d tried all the toothpastes and other products, and had gotten no further along.
