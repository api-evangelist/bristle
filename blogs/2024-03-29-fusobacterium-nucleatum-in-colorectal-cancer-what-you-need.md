It’s been known for nearly a decade that the oral bacterium, Fusobacterium nucleatum (Fn) , is found enriched in Colorectal Cancer (CRC) Tumors. Higher abundance of Fn in CRC tumors is associated with recurrence, metastases, and poorer patient prognosis. So should this bacterium be eliminated from the mouth at all costs?
