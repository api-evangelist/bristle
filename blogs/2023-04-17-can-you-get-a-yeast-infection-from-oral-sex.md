While oral sex is a great way to increase intimacy with your partner, there are few things you should know! While it might seem less risky than other types of sexual activity, the reality is, there’s a risk of transmission of infection or disease with oral sex similar to traditional intercourse. And we aren’t here to be a buzzkill but we want you to understand there is a chance that yeast infections can be spread through oral sex, whether you are giving or receiving.
